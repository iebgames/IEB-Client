Installation Instructions

Follow these quick steps to set up the client jar on your system:

  • Make sure Minecraft Forge 1.8.9 is installed on your launcher.
  • Press Win + R on your keyboard, type %appdata% and hit Enter.
  • Navigate into the .minecraft folder, then open the mods folder.
  • Place the downloaded .jar file directly inside the mods folder.
  • Restart your game and press TAB to open the menu.

File Safety & Verification

All compiled releases of IEB Client are open-source. You can audit the complete compilation scripts, shadows, and Mixins directly in our repository. The files are checked and built automatically using GitHub Actions build chains to verify safety and build integrity.
